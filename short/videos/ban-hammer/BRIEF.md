## Intent

A 30 second viral Roblox YouTube Short, directed by Claude. High-stakes storyline
(unreleased admin item), fast cuts every 1 to 1.5 seconds locked to the beat,
screen shakes on impacts, shift-lock camera zooms (1.5x to 2x), speed ramps and snap
transforms. Nothing is ever fully still. Cold open hooks in the first 0.8 seconds,
rapid escalation, chaotic climax on the drop, ending loops seamlessly into the start.

## Customizations

- Everything on screen is code: HTML, CSS, SVG, canvas, GSAP. No images, no stock
  footage, no generated media.
- Soundtrack synthesized with the Web Audio API, rendered offline with
  OfflineAudioContext to a WAV (no samples or audio files), then muxed into the MP4:
  sub booms, filtered-noise risers, synthesized Roblox "oof", rhythmic phonk beat,
  massive drop on the climax. Loud but never clipping or crushed.
- Output: 1080x1920, 60fps MP4.

## Notes

- Palette: electric yellow #FFD700, panic red #FF1E27, cyan glow #00FFFF, obsidian
  background #0F0F12. Extra-heavy kinetic type with black outlines.
- Text is sparse and big: a few words per moment, never a paragraph. One idea at a
  time; nothing flashes by before it can be read.
